The Doctrine

Four articles of the regime

Everything below is literally true. The jokes are the only propaganda.

Fully offline

Speech recognition runs entirely on your phone. No internet connection is used for transcription, so it works on a plane, in a tunnel, or in a bunker.

“The regime cannot intercept what never leaves the phone.”

Privacy first

Your voice and your text are yours. Nothing is sent to a server for processing, and there is no cloud account standing between you and your own words.

“Trust no one. Especially not a data center.”

Many languages

Speak the language you think in. Master Dictator understands many languages and writes them down properly, punctuation included. More are on the way.

“It understands you in many languages. It obeys you in all of them.”

Works in every app

It is a real keyboard. Messaging, email, notes, forms: wherever you can type, you can dictate. No copy-pasting from a separate app.

“Every text field is sovereign territory.”

How it works

Three steps to absolute power over your keyboard

  1. Hold the microphone key

    Master Dictator sits where your keyboard normally is. Tap the red key and start talking. No app switching, no clipboard gymnastics.

  2. Speak in your language

    Talk normally, at normal speed, in the language you actually think in. Switch languages mid-conversation if you like.

  3. Your text appears. Nothing else happens.

    The words show up in the text field, properly punctuated. The audio never leaves the phone. There is no step four.

Questions from the Masses

Frequently asked questions

Does my voice leave my phone?

No. Speech recognition runs on the device itself. There is no server doing the work for you and no recording being shipped anywhere for "quality purposes".

Which languages does it understand?

Many, and the list is growing as new recognition models are added. Punctuation and capitalisation are produced automatically.

When can I get it?

Master Dictator is coming soon to Google Play. This page will carry the download link the moment it is live. Until then, this repository is the place to watch.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?

On the issue tracker. Every petition to the Politburo is read. Most are even answered.

Why the dictators?

Because a dictator is someone who dictates, and the other kind of dictator never let anyone listen in on him. Master Dictator gives you the second thing without the first. Satire, in case that needed saying.
