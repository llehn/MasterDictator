# App icon

The Master Dictator icon: a leader behind a burning microphone, with a crowd in front of him. Everything except the microphone is misty steel blue, and the microphone is the only fire in the picture.

The same scene comes in two levels of detail. Small sizes need a shape you can recognise at a glance, and large sizes need detail.

## Files

| File | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `icon_small.svg` | 108×108, the Android adaptive-icon grid. Heavy dark outline on the microphone, little detail | Launcher icon and anything shown at roughly 128 px or smaller |
| `icon_large.svg` | 512×512, full detail: podium, clouds, a larger crowd, a studio microphone with a mesh grille | Large uses where detail pays off. Not the app or Play icon |
| `icon_launcher.svg` | The small icon cut to the centre 72×72 a launcher shows, square, with the sky | The website's logo (header and poster) |
| `favicon.svg` | The small icon zoomed in so the cap almost touches the top edge, square, transparent background (no sky, fog or grain) | The website's browser tab icon |
| `png/icon_small_<px>.png` | `icon_small.svg` rendered at 108, 162, 216, 324 and 432 px: the 108 dp adaptive-icon grid at mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi and xxxhdpi | The Android launcher icon |
| `png/icon_play_512.png` | The small icon cut to the centre 72×72 a launcher shows, rendered at 512×512 | The Play Store listing icon, so the store shows what the phone shows |

In `icon_small.svg`, launchers only show the centre 72×72 and cut it to their own shape (circle, squircle, rounded square). Keep anything important inside the centre circle of diameter 66.

All four SVGs use SVG filters (blur, rim light, and grain in the icons). Android's VectorDrawable cannot express these, so the Android and Play Store assets are the PNGs in `png/`.

## Android

Copy `png/icon_small_<px>.png` to `res/mipmap-<density>/ic_launcher_foreground.png` (108 → mdpi, 162 → hdpi, 216 → xhdpi, 324 → xxhdpi, 432 → xxxhdpi) and use it as the foreground of an adaptive icon. Copy `png/icon_play_512.png` to the Play listing's icon.

## Changing the artwork

Don't edit the SVGs by hand. They are generated by `tools/icon/generate.js`:

```
node tools/icon/generate.js
```

This rewrites all four SVGs and the PNGs in this folder. It needs Node.js, plus Microsoft Edge or Chrome for the PNGs (set `BROWSER` to the executable's path if it is not found).
